i Scope & limits

    

      This tool reconciles data and surfaces exceptions for a human to act on. By design it does
      not:
      
    
        
  • decide your policy — you configure the training rule; it then checks it accurately;
    • 
        
  • grant or revoke access — it flags a mismatch and an administrator investigates;
    • 
        
  • send anything anywhere — files are parsed locally and never leave the browser.
